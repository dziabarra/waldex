## About the project

A stand for **Master Colors**, a distributor of machines and components for plastics processing, at **Plastpol 2026** in Kielce. 80 m² in Hall 3, corner location.

Form and finish:

- **Spatial logotype** with internally backlit letters, visible from the main aisles
- **Openwork wooden slats** screening the meeting area from the display zone
- **Wood plank flooring** across the whole stand
- **Partner branding walls**: technotrans, MORETTO, BREYER, SIEMPELKAMP, EAS, DEIFEL, orcinus, Cycle Temp, gneuß
- **Lounge area** with soft seating and warm spot lighting

## Display

Temperature management and thermal control units sat at the front of the stand, connected and shown running. Behind the slats, a separate, quieter zone for client conversations, kept away from the traffic in the product area.

Everything handled on our side: design, manufacturing, graphics, transport, installation and dismantling.
