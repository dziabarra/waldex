## About the project

A stand designed for **Master Colors** — a distributor of machines and components for plastics processing — for **Plastpol 2025** in Kielce (the largest plastics processing trade fair in Poland).

**Dimensions and form:**

- **Area: 80 m²** (8 × 10 m, corner location)
- **4-meter central tower** (2.76 × 2.76 × 4 m module) visible from every aisle in the hall — the brand anchor
- **9 partner branding walls**: MORETTO (auxiliary equipment), technotrans (thermal management), BREYER (extrusion lines), SIEMPELKAMP, EAS (Quick Mold Change), DEIFEL (Buntfarbenfabrik), orcinus, Cycle Temp, gneuß
- **Spatial Master Colors logotype** CNC-milled from PVC-XPS, internally backlit with RGB LED
- **Modular aluminum construction** — Octanorm system allowing the stand to be reconfigured for subsequent fair editions

## Process

From client brief to a finished stand on opening day — **9 weeks**:

1. Concept design and 3D visualizations in four shape variants
2. Client approval of the shape and materials
3. Manufacturing at our facility (CNC, large-format printing, spatial logotype assembly)
4. Transport to Targi Kielce
5. On-site installation (2 days before opening)
6. Dismantling and warehousing of reusable elements after the fair
