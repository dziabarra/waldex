## About the project

A stand designed for **Master Colors** — a distributor of machines and components for plastics processing — for **Plastpol 2025** in Kielce (the largest plastics processing trade fair in Poland).

**Dimensions and form:**

- **Area: 80 m²** (8 × 10 m, corner location)
- **4-meter central tower** (2.76 × 2.76 × 4 m module) visible from every aisle in the hall — the brand anchor
- **9 partner branding walls**: MORETTO (auxiliary equipment), technotrans (thermal management), BREYER (extrusion lines), SIEMPELKAMP, EAS (Quick Mold Change), DEIFEL (Buntfarbenfabrik), orcinus, Cycle Temp, gneuß
- **Spatial Master Colors logotype** CNC-milled from PVC-XPS, internally backlit with RGB LED
- **Modular aluminum construction** — Octanorm system allowing the stand to be reconfigured for subsequent fair editions

## Hall plan and dimensioning

Corner stand 8×10 m in the heart of the Plastpol hall, neighboring PIC, Prosperplast, ROSCO and a smaller Master Colors stand (18 m²). The 4-meter central tower was planned to be visible from every aisle — a brand anchor for Master Colors in the context of the 9 partner brand display.

Single tower module: **2.76 × 2.76 × 4 m**. Each of the four walls carries a different partner brand (MORETTO, technotrans, BREYER, EAS), creating a rotational effect — from every side of the hall the stand tells a different story.

## 3D concepts

The client received **10 variants of 3D visualization** differing in spatial layout, product display, and partner branding combinations. Renderings in full scene lighting helped approve the final form before any material was cut.

## Realization process

From client brief to a finished stand on opening day — **9 weeks**:

1. **Hall plan and floor approval** — coordination with the Plastpol organizer, neighbor selection, approval of 8×10 m × 4 m shape
2. **3D concepts** — 10 visualization variants, final selection by the client
3. **Graphic design** — 4 wall graphics (2500×4000 and 2600×4000 mm) with Master Colors branding + 9 partner brands
4. **Manufacturing** — Octanorm aluminum tower, UV-printed Dibond panels, CNC-milled spatial logotypes from PVC-XPS
5. **Transport, installation and dismantling** — slot 2 days before opening, Polish technical crew on-site, warehousing of modules for the next edition
