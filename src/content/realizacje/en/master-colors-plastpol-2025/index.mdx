## About the project

A trade fair stand designed for **Master Colors** — a manufacturer of plastics colorants — for **Plastpol 2025** in Kielce (the largest plastics processing trade fair in Poland).

Key project assumptions:

- **Central product display** — a glass showcase with colorant samples in the full chromatic range, backlit from below to intensify saturation
- **Customer conversation area** — a dedicated space at the information counter, soft 3000 K lighting
- **Visual identity** — a 60 cm tall spatial Master Colors logotype, manufactured from graphically laminated PVC
- **Modular construction** — aluminum system allowing the stand to be reconfigured for subsequent fair editions

## Process

From client brief to a finished stand on opening day — **9 weeks**:

1. Concept design and 3D visualizations in four shape variants
2. Client approval of the shape and materials
3. Manufacturing at Waldex's facility (CNC, large-format printing, logotype assembly)
4. Transport to Targi Kielce
5. On-site installation (2 days before opening)
6. Dismantling and warehousing of reusable elements after the fair
