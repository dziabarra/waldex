## About the project

A small, budget 16 m² stand for **Insotech** (industrial cooling systems) at **Plastpol 2026** in Kielce. A corner build where the graphics do most of the work.

The solution:

- **Area 16 m²**, corner layout
- **Large-format graphics** printed on fabric tensioned into frames (a textile system — light and flat)
- **Grey carpet** throughout
- **Spot lighting** on the top rail
- Clear content split: technotrans (cooling and thermal control), the Insotech logo, and the ChillerRent and Chiller Serwis brands

A good example that a stand doesn't have to cost much to look right and sell.
