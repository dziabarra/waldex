## About the project

A stand designed for **BSG** for **FSB 2023** in Cologne — one of Europe's largest trade fairs for the sports, leisure, and pool industries. FSB runs every 2 years and gathers exhibitors from 50+ countries.

Project assumptions:

- **Open layout** on three sides — maximum product visibility from any aisle
- **Wooden structural elements** — warm, natural look fitting the sport/lifestyle segment
- **Functional zones** — client conversations, product display, reception, back-office
- **Professional LED lighting** — directional on products, diffused in the conversation area

## EU logistics

FSB is an international event — full logistics from our Polish facility to Koelnmesse:

- Modular transport in ATA-carnet-compatible flight cases
- Polish installation crew on-site (3 people, 2 days of build)
- Installation slot reserved 2 months before opening
- Vehicle passes for installation crew registered via the organizer's portal
