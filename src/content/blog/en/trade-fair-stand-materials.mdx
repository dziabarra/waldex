Every material has its place. A good contractor mixes them per the specific stand, not pushes one solution into everything. Below — what we actually pick, in which situations.

## Load-bearing structure

The skeleton — invisible but it determines stability, build time, and reusability.

### Modular aluminum (Octanorm, Maxima, R8)

Aluminum profile with click connectors. **Industry standard** for most stands under 100 m².

- **Pros:** fast build (4-12h for 30-50 m²), reusable (8-12 years), cheap refurbishment, wide accessory range
- **Cons:** visible aluminum profiles at edges (cross), limited bespoke angles, same look as competitors
- **Cost:** ~€35-70/m² of stand area for the system itself

**Pick when:** serial exhibits (3+ per year), area 9-50 m², tight budget.

### Welded steel structure

Frame from steel profiles, usually for **large stands with mezzanines**. Requires structural certification.

- **Pros:** unique shapes, mezzanines without limits, premium feel
- **Cons:** heavy, expensive transport, not reusable, requires certification

**Pick when:** stands 80+ m², with mezzanine, for flagship fairs.

### Glued wood / OSB

Cheap structure for small pop-up stands or events.

- **Pros:** fastest to design and build, natural look, can be glued and cut on-site
- **Cons:** heavier than aluminum, single-use (falls apart after transport)

**Pick when:** event stands, pop-ups, consumer fairs under 25 m², when it's a one-off.

## Wall surfaces

The "skin" of the stand — the most visible part for visitors.

### Printed PVC panels (3-5 mm)

- **Pros:** cheap (~€12-25/m²), easy to install, full color print, lightweight
- **Cons:** looks like plastic up close, fingerprints show, scratches easily

**Ideal for:** entire side walls, large-format photo walls, branding backgrounds.

### Dibond (aluminum-PVC-aluminum composite)

- **Pros:** rigid, holds shape, looks premium, prints well, **3-4× more expensive than PVC** but the look is 5× better
- **Cons:** higher weight (3.5-4 kg/m²), doesn't bend
- **Cost:** ~€47-82/m² with print

**Ideal for:** spatial logotypes (flat letters), front panels, backlit niches.

### HPL laminate (High-Pressure Laminate)

- **Pros:** **hardest surface** of all (very hard to scratch), wide pattern range (wood, stone, metal), 5+ years of durability
- **Cons:** expensive (~€58-105/m²), not for printing
- **We use it for:** counters, tabletops, displays, anywhere clients touch

### Lacquered MDF

- **Pros:** elegant smooth finish, bespoke angles (milled), beautiful "showroom" look
- **Cons:** lacquering needs 2-3 days drying, doesn't tolerate humidity, requires gentle transport
- **Cost:** ~€95-165/m² (with 3-coat lacquer)

**Ideal for:** premium custom stands, reception fronts, half-height zone dividers.

### Veneered / solid wood

Picked by brands wanting **"natural / craft / premium"** look.

- **Pros:** unique look, texture, "warmth" — very good for organic premium brands
- **Cons:** most expensive option (veneers €140-280/m², solid wood €350-700/m²), sensitive to hall climate

## Lighting

The most underrated element — often **the difference between "nice" and "wow"**.

### LED strips in profiles

- **Pros:** hidden in aluminum, even diffusion, easy install
- **Cost:** ~€70-140/linear meter with profile

**Used for:** counter underlighting, in-display product lighting, graphic accents from behind.

### Trade fair spotlights

- **Pros:** directional, swappable, easy to aim after install
- **Cost:** €45-115 each depending on power
- **Watts:** 20-30W per 1.5-2 m² of lit surface

**Watch the color temperature:** 3000K (warm) for premium / lifestyle brands, 4000K (neutral) for B2B / industrial, 5000K+ (cold) — we don't use it, looks like a hospital.

### Backlit spatial letters

3D logotype with LEDs inside — **a classic for visibility from a distance**. Costs €350-1,800 depending on size and material.

## Floor

- **Trade fair carpet** — standard, organizer usually provides in the base fee. We recommend it only for budget stands.
- **Click vinyl panels** — wood imitation, easy install, ~€19-35/m². Mid-tier stand standard.
- **Layered wood / veneers** — premium feel, but **expensive** (~€70-115/m²) and scratch-sensitive.
- **Cut-shape carpet** — for stands with podium. Black, gray, beige — color strongly affects acoustics and perception of cleanliness.

## What we at Waldex typically recommend

For a typical 30-50 m² B2B stand, premium but rational budget:

- **Structure:** modular Octanorm aluminum
- **Side walls:** 3 mm printed Dibond
- **Counter / top:** HPL on MDF (core) with laminated printed graphics
- **Lighting:** LED strips in profiles + 4-6 spotlights 25W on product
- **Logotype:** Dibond spatial letters, backlit
- **Floor:** vinyl panels, light oak imitation

This combination gives **80% of full custom effect at 60% of the price**. Works well for 90% of B2B stands in central Europe.
