We are opening this blog for a simple reason — most companies commissioning their first trade fair stand don't know what to look at. **Price is one factor among several**, not the most important. What matters is timeliness, reusability of the construction, the quality of details (lighting, graphics, panel joints).

Topics we plan to cover in the coming months:

- The **real** cost of a trade fair stand — price ranges by m², materials, and the fair
- **Modular vs custom stand** — when to choose which, and how to calculate ROI
- **The exhibitor's brief** — what information to prepare before the first conversation with a contractor
- **EU fair logistics** — what to expect for Hannover Messe / Milan trips
- What to do when **the stand won't be ready in time** — plan B and who to call first

Every post with concrete numbers, no marketing fluff. When we don't know something, or it's "it depends" — we say so plainly.

Welcome. New posts are linked in the footer, and the [full RSS](/en/rss.xml) is here for readers who prefer feed readers.
