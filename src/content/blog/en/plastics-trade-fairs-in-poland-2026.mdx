The plastics industry in Poland has several key exhibition events. Below — a practical 2026 calendar with dates, locations, exhibitor and visitor profiles, and what to watch for when planning your stand.

## Plastpol — International Fair of Plastics and Rubber Processing

**The most important industry event in Poland.**

- **2026 date:** 26-29 May 2026
- **Location:** Targi Kielce
- **Size:** ~750 exhibitors from 30 countries, 16,000+ visitors, ~25,000 m² of exhibition space
- **Exhibitor profile:** plastic producers, processing machines, injection molds, tooling, recycling, additives
- **Visitor profile:** mostly B2B — technologists, production managers, procurement from processing companies
- **2026 floor pricing:** ~€88/m² net (raw floor), ~€125/m² with organizer's standard build

**Things to know when planning your stand:**
- Halls G, H, F, E — best to ask the organizer which industry profile clusters where
- Installation slot usually 24-25 May (Sunday-Monday)
- Heavy machinery logistics (extruders, injection machines) requires coordination with the organizer 2 months in advance
- Live machine demos — very effective, but require permits for noise and ventilation

## Plastex — Plastics Trade Fair in Sosnowiec

**Second-largest event in Poland, strong presence of Silesian chemical companies.**

- **2026 date:** second half of September (typically 22-24 September)
- **Location:** Trade Fair and Conference Center, Sosnowiec
- **Size:** ~150-200 exhibitors, ~5,000 visitors
- **Profile:** fewer machines, more engineering and specialty plastics
- **Atmosphere:** smaller, more intimate than Plastpol — easier 1:1 conversations with technologists

**For whom:** raw material producers, distributors, chemical companies. Less for machines, more for materials.

## Komposyt-Expo — Composite Materials Trade Fair

**Niche but growing — important for companies working with laminates and reinforced composites.**

- **2026 date:** October (typically 14-15 October)
- **Location:** Kraków (Targi w Krakowie)
- **Size:** ~80-120 exhibitors, ~3,000 visitors
- **Profile:** glass/carbon fiber reinforced composites, aerospace, automotive, wind energy

## International Plastics and Rubber Fair in Poznań

**Smaller, but good for regional companies.**

- **2026 date:** March / April (check with organizer)
- **Location:** MTP Poznań
- **Size:** ~50-100 exhibitors
- **Profile:** local Wielkopolska companies + distributors

## "Occasional" events — Plast360, Consumer Plastics

Depending on the year, smaller fairs come and go. Check:

- **Plast360** — every 2 years, focus on consumer plastics (packaging, household appliances)
- **Polagra-Premiery** in Poznań — mostly food, but the plastic packaging section is always present
- **Krakdent** (dental fair) — niche, but medical plastic companies show up

## How to pick which fairs are right for you

Criteria, in order:

### 1. Customer geography

Plastpol = international (Germany, Czechia, Italy, Ukraine). Plastex = mostly Poland + Central Europe. Poznań Plast = local Wielkopolska.

### 2. Your company size vs competition

Small firms often get lost at Plastpol next to massive expos. Plastex gives **better contacts/spend ratio** for SMEs.

### 3. Industry seasonality

Industrial plastic — bought year-round. Packaging plastic — peaks in spring/autumn (before consumer seasons).

### 4. What you're already planning at other fairs

If you're exhibiting at Drupa (Düsseldorf, German 2027) or K Show (Düsseldorf, plastics 2025) — no point duplicating the budget on Plastex the same year. Better split: 2 large + 1 niche events.
