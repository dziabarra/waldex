Every exhibitor sooner or later faces the same choice. **Modular system** (Octanorm, Maxima, octametric aluminum profile) — fast, cheaper, easy to change layout. **Custom stand** — wood, lacquer, bespoke shapes, more expensive, looks better. Which one?

Short answer: **modular when you exhibit 3+ times a year or your budget is under €8,000 for a 30 m² stand. Custom when once a year, you need a wow effect and premium look, and you have €11,500+ to spend.**

The longer answer depends on five things.

## 1. Exhibition frequency

A modular system pays off **on the second use**. The structure stays, you only swap the graphics. The second edition typically costs 30-40% of the first (just panel swap + transport + installation). The third is even cheaper.

Custom usually **disassembles and goes back to warehouse in pieces**, but every next edition needs refurbishment, repainting, replacing damaged elements. Realistically: 50-60% of the first price on the second use.

| Number of fairs | Modular | Custom |
|---|---|---|
| 1× | even chances | if budget permits — wow effect |
| 2-3× | ⭐ modular | custom more expensive |
| 4+× | modular wins decisively | custom too costly |

## 2. Look & feel

Custom usually wins **at first sight**. Wood, smooth lacquered surfaces, bespoke angles, mezzanines — modular won't do that (or will, but the aluminum edges show).

BUT: a well-designed modular with thick printed graphics + good LED lighting + custom furniture can **look like custom from 3 meters away**. This is the practical truth competitors won't tell you — because they'd rather sell you more expensive custom.

## 3. Build time on the fair floor

Modular: typically **4-12 hours** for 30-50 m². You connect profiles, click in panels, plug in LEDs. Custom: **2-4 days** for the same area — wood needs on-site lacquering, hand-finished trim, more elements to assemble.

This matters when the fair has **1-2 days for installation**. In Poland it's usually relaxed. In Hannover and Düsseldorf, build slots get tight — modular saves your skin.

## 4. Design lead time

Modular: 2-3 weeks from brief to production. Custom: 4-8 weeks (more visualization rounds, wood and lacquer selection, prototypes).

If the fair is **6 weeks away** — custom probably won't make it, unless rushed (and expensive).

## 5. Reusability / lifecycle

Modular aluminum profiles last **8-12 years** without replacement under normal use. You only swap graphics and furniture. Excellent economics for serial exhibitors.

A wooden custom stand has **3-5 years** before requiring serious refurbishment. Lacquer wears, wood cracks during transport, edges get bumped. This isn't a flaw of custom — it's the nature of the material.

## Who each variant is for

**Modular is for you if:**
- You exhibit **3+ times a year** across different cities
- Stand area is typically **9-50 m²**
- You value **predictability** of time and budget
- Your industry refreshes products/messaging every 1-2 years (you can swap only graphics)
- The fairs you attend have **tight installation slots**

**Custom is for you if:**
- You exhibit **1-2 times a year**, but these are **flagship events** (Hannover Messe, Plastpol, ITM)
- Premium brand where the stand **has to look like a showroom, not flat-packed Ikea**
- Area 50+ m² where small finishing differences are visible from across the hall
- Budget allows (for 50 m² we're talking €18,500-35,000 net)
- You have **8+ weeks** from brief to opening

## Hybrid — often-overlooked option

In practice, we build a lot of **hybrid stands**: modular skeleton + custom frontages and furniture. This gives **70-80% of the custom wow effect at 130% of modular price** instead of 200%+ of full custom.

Hybrid works particularly well at 30-60 m². Wow points (counter, lighting, 3D graphics, wall accent) done custom; structural side walls — modular.
