The question we get most often from first-time exhibitors: **how much does this all cost?** "It depends" is true, but unhelpful. So in this post we give concrete ranges for 2026 and explain **what determines whether you fall in the lower or upper bracket**.

## Quick summary — price ranges

| Area | Modular stand | Custom stand | Premium custom |
|---|---|---|---|
| **9 m² (3×3)** | €1,800 – 3,500 | €4,000 – 7,000 | €8,000+ |
| **18 m² (3×6)** | €3,500 – 7,000 | €7,000 – 14,000 | €15,000+ |
| **30 m² (5×6)** | €5,800 – 10,500 | €10,500 – 21,000 | €23,000+ |
| **48 m² (6×8)** | €9,300 – 16,300 | €16,300 – 32,500 | €37,000+ |
| **80 m² (8×10)** | €15,100 – 28,000 | €25,500 – 51,200 | €58,000+ |
| **150+ m²** | €28,000+ | €46,500+ | €93,000+ |

These are **net, no VAT, for 2026**, on the central European market (Poland baseline). Foreign fairs (Hannover, Milan, Düsseldorf) typically add 30-60% for logistics and local crews.

## What goes into the price

Clients usually see one number in the quote. In reality, it breaks down into five line items:

### 1. Design and visualizations (5-10%)

Designer's work from brief to finished 3D visualizations. The more bespoke the shape, the more revision rounds. For a typical 30 m² stand: €700-1,800.

### 2. Materials and manufacturing (40-55%)

This is where most of the budget sits:

- **Structure** — modular aluminum (Octanorm/Maxima) is cheaper; welded steel, glued wood, or OSB build is more expensive
- **Surfaces** — printed PVC/Dibond panels, HPL laminates, glass, lacquered MDF
- **Lighting** — LED strips (€70-140/m), spotlights (€45-115 each), full-spectrum daylight panels
- **Furniture** — fair-rental (€70-350/piece) vs custom-built for the stand
- **Spatial logotypes** — €350-1,800 depending on size and material
- **AV** — TVs (€115-580 rental), interactive displays, projections

### 3. Transport and warehousing (8-15%)

Domestic transport: €350-930 by distance and volume. International: €930-3,500. Warehousing of modular elements until the next edition: €45-115/m³/month.

### 4. Installation and dismantling (10-18%)

On-site crew, usually 2-4 people for 1-3 days. Hourly rate €19-42 per person. **This cost grows linearly** with build time — large premium stands with wood and finishing take 4-5 days.

### 5. Fair fees (separate from the stand)

You pay these to the organizer, not the stand builder. For reference: exhibition fee at Plastpol 2026 is around €88/m² plus registration, electricity, internet fees. These costs **should not appear in the contractor's quote** — if they do, ask why.

## What raises the price

Five things that most often push the budget up:

1. **Mezzanine (second floor)** — adds 30-50% over the base. Requires certified construction, stairs, railings, sometimes a personal lift.
2. **Custom furniture** — bespoke pieces (counters, displays, branded sofas) can add €3,500-9,300.
3. **Multilayer graphics** — embossed printed elements, backlit spatial lettering, large-format high-resolution photo walls.
4. **Tight deadline** — 4-5 weeks before the fair instead of 8-10 means a 15-30% rush surcharge.
5. **Foreign fairs** — destination country VAT (Italy 22%), local installation crews (sometimes 2× the Polish rate), customs fees, certifications.

## What lowers the price

Three real ways:

1. **Modular system instead of custom** — 40-60% difference at similar visual quality from a distance. Ideal when you're exhibiting at 3-4 events per year and want a reusable stand.
2. **Early brief** — for the contractor, 10 weeks vs 5 weeks is the difference between calm production planning and overtime surcharges.
3. **Reusing elements from a previous edition** — if you already had a stand, keeping the structure and only swapping graphics is typically 30-50% of a new stand's cost.

## What to avoid when picking a contractor

Three red flags we see in competitors' quotes:

- **"Price on request"** — without even a range. A professional contractor gives you a quick quote with assumptions in 1-2 business days.
- **No line-item breakdown** — "50 m² stand — €18,500" without breakdown of materials, design, installation. Always ask for line items.
- **No reuse offer** — if the contractor doesn't ask about your previous stands and doesn't propose reusing elements, the savings go into their margin, not your budget.
