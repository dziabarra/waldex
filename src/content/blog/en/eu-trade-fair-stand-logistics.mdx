Domestic fairs are a walk in the park compared to going abroad. The logistics of a stand at Hannover Messe, K Show in Düsseldorf, or MICAM in Milan can **double the total cost** vs a Polish event of the same area. Below — what really costs, what to watch for, what to avoid.

## The three biggest costs of going abroad

### 1. Material transport

Poland → Germany (Hannover, Düsseldorf, Frankfurt): **€820-1,650** for a 22 m³ tarp truck with Polish driver.

Poland → Italy (Milan, Bologna): **€1,400-2,800**, longer transport + tolls + potential border waits.

Poland → Spain, Portugal, southern France: **€2,300-4,200**, plus driver accommodation.

What affects the price:
- Volume (m³) — the number that rules, not weight
- Seasonality — spring and autumn (peak fair season) can add +30%
- Two-way trips — you save by finding a return cargo (booked through freight exchanges)

### 2. Local installation crew vs Polish

**Polish crew sent to Germany:** daily rate €140-210/person (per-diem + hotel + travel) × 2-4 days. Plus Polish health insurance EHIC.

**Local German crew:** €60-90/h × 8h × 2-3 people × 2 days = €2,880-4,320 (~€2,800-4,200).

**Rule:** for stands **up to 50 m²**, sending a Polish crew pays (cheaper + better communication). Above 80 m², a local crew starts to make sense due to limited installation slots.

### 3. Destination country VAT and taxes

The biggest trap for first-year exhibitors.

**Germany (19% VAT):** A Polish exhibitor can reclaim VAT through cross-border refund (EU-13), but it takes **6-12 months**. Meanwhile, you freeze working capital.

**Italy (22% VAT):** refund takes **8-18 months**, documentation requires a local tax advisor.

**Optimization:** invoice through a holding company registered in the destination country (if you have one). Or use a Polish stand contractor who **issues a Polish invoice** but delivers the stand abroad — then VAT is Polish 23% and deducts normally.

## Build slots — how to plan ahead

Major EU fairs have **very tight slots**:

- **Hannover Messe** — install 3 days before opening, dismantle within 24h after closing. **No flexibility.**
- **K Show Düsseldorf** (every 3 years, plastics) — 5 days install, but heavy machinery exhibitors get dedicated "early access"
- **MICAM Milan** (fashion) — 2 days install, end-of-day dismantle (very tight)
- **Drupa Düsseldorf** (printing) — 2 weeks install (one of the longest in the EU, for very large installations)

**Rule:** check the organizer's slot **BEFORE** signing with a contractor. If a Polish contractor needs 4 days of install but the slot is 2 — you have a problem.

## Vehicle passes

Germany and Italy **do not let unmarked trucks into the fair hall** without prior registration. Procedure:

1. Exhibitor registers vehicles in the organizer's portal (usually 2-4 weeks ahead)
2. Receives "vehicle passes" PDF with QR code
3. Driver shows the pass at the hall gate

**No registration = truck waits in an external lot**, materials carried by hand. Add +6h to install time.

## Insurance

Polish auto insurance continues in the EU (Green Card not needed since 2017). But **the trade fair stand** is not covered by standard company liability.

Real option: exhibition insurance from the organizer (usually 0.3-0.6% of stand value) or separate fair insurance from a Polish insurer (~€115-350 per event).

**What's worth insuring:**
- Theft of tools and presentation gear (laptops, screens, swag) — most common incidents
- Damage to the stand during transport
- Liability for damages caused to third parties (visitors)

## Crew catering / accommodation

Small but real cost: a 4-person installation crew working 2-3 days at a foreign fair **needs to eat**. Hotel + per-diem + daily meal:

- Hotel: €80-150/night/person near the fair (Hannover in peak season can go higher)
- Per-diem per person: €35-50/day (breakfast + lunch + dinner)
- Total for 4-person crew, 3 days: ~€700-1,150

This is a line item **often skipped in the contractor's quote**. Ask straight up: "is the crew accommodation and per-diems in the price?"

## Returns and post-fair invoices

After the fair, returns/invoices show up that you didn't expect:

- **Non-returned organizer items become your property** — some organizers rent panels and if you return them damaged, they invoice you
- **Left-behind media** (electronics in displays) — your responsibility for return shipping
- **Catering / parking / passes** — often invoiced AFTER the fair, sometimes 30-60 days later

**Budget plan:** keep 10-15% reserve for "post-fair" costs.
